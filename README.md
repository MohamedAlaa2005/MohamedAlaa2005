      <h3>🌍 Deimos</h3>
      <b>Earth Analog Discovery for the Moon & Mars</b>

      <p>
        A planetary data engineering and similarity-search platform
        that identifies Earth locations resembling selected lunar
        or Martian regions.
      </p>

      <ul>
        <li>Integrates scientific datasets across three planetary bodies.</li>
        <li>Standardizes spatial features on a common 1° × 1° grid.</li>
        <li>Ranks the Top 5 Earth analog locations.</li>
        <li>Built with FastAPI and Neon PostgreSQL.</li>
      </ul>
